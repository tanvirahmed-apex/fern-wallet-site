Fern Wallet

Your money, kept on your phone.

Fern Wallet is a personal finance app for Android. Track expenses, income and transfers across accounts, plan budgets and recurring payments, and keep loans in check. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Offline first Everything is stored in a database on your device.
Budgets & planning Budgets, planned payments, reports and charts.
Optional Drive backup Back up to a private app folder in your own Google Drive.
App lock Protect the app with your fingerprint or screen lock.

Google Drive backup

If you turn on Drive backup, Fern Wallet asks for access to its own app data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). It can't see or change any of your other Drive files. Read the privacy policy for details.

Fern Wallet

Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Fern Wallet ("the app") is a personal finance app for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your financial data stays on your device, unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive or export it yourself.

Data stored on your device

Accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, loans, planned payments and settings are stored in a local database on your device. The developer has no server and never receives this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google to turn on Drive backup:

  • The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This gives it access only to a hidden folder that belongs to Fern Wallet in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • The app uploads backup files of your Fern Wallet data to that folder, and reads them back when you restore. Backups go directly from your device to Google; the developer never receives them.
  • The app reads your Google account's email address from Google Drive and shows it so you know which account is connected. It is kept on your device only.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the app, or remove Fern Wallet's access in your Google Account settings. You can delete the backup data from Google Drive's settings under "Manage apps".

Fern Wallet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the backup feature, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone.

Exchange rates

To convert between currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from a free currency API (currency-api.pages.dev, with cdn.jsdelivr.net as a fallback). These requests contain no personal or financial data. Like any web request, they reveal your IP address to those services.

Files you export

When you export a backup or a CSV file, or import one, the app reads or writes only the file you pick. What happens to an exported file after that is up to you.

Device features

  • App lock uses Android's biometric or screen-lock prompt. The app never sees your fingerprint.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • Home screen widgets read data from the app's local database.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
